Play through side-scrolling fights, on-beat stage challenges, an amp rescue, party games, and a final dive-bar Battle of the Bands. Bad promoters, drunk fans, and rival musicians have all decided to become your problem.

## Play

Open the **website link in this repository's About panel**. Choose **Start the Game**, then power on the amp.

Use a desktop or laptop browser with a keyboard. No installer is required. The homepage lives at `index.html`; the game runs from `game.html`.

## The band

| Player | Instrument | Combat loadout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matt | Bass guitar | Bass guitar |
| Brice | Lead guitar and vocals | Lead guitar |
| Tony | Drums | Drumsticks |

In the final battle, press **X** to switch musicians. Your band shares a health meter.

## Tonight's setlist

1. **Practice:** match graffiti arrows on the beat in the rehearsal room.
2. **Sound Check:** play your part, then fix the mix.
3. **The Show:** perform and fight off stage trouble.
4. **The Amp Blew:** collect a spare amp from the van and bring it back.
5. **Groupie Scorecard:** a random lucky choice grants giant size and one-hit attacks.
6. **After Party:** Beer Pong and **Blowin' O's — Smoke Ring Olympics**.
7. **Battle of the Bands:** defeat thirty rival musicians. Losing the final battle gets you kicked out of the band.

## Main controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD or arrows | Move during action sections |
| J / K / L | Strike / Heavy strike / Dash |
| Space | Jump; timed release in party games |
| E | Interact / Confirm |
| P / M | Pause / Toggle music |
| X | Switch musicians during the final battle |
| Arrows or A/S/D/F | Rhythm notes: Left / Down / Up / Right |

Controls change by activity. Follow the instructions inside the game. Beer Pong uses left/right aim; Blowin' O's uses up/down aim. Press Space when direction and power line up.

## Notes

This edition has no mid-level Save/Load. Reloading or closing the page does not preserve a run in progress. Chapter Select lets you revisit sections.

Contains strong language, cartoon violence, suggestive humor, alcohol, and smoking/cannabis references.

The music starts after you power on the amp. If the game pauses after changing windows, click back into it and press **P**.
