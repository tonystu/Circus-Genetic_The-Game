CIRCUS GENETIC THE GAME

http://circusgenetic.game/

Windows desktop demo, version 1.0.0. Panta Rhei Productions.

Start a band. Survive the gig. Keep the beer off the equipment.

Play as Matt, Brice or Tony through rehearsals, bad gigs, an amp emergency, party games and a final bar-room Battle of the Bands.

# Installation

Built for 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11.

1. Right-click **Circus-Genetic-Windows-Installer.zip** and choose **Extract All**.

2. Open the extracted folder and run **Circus-Genetic-Setup-1.0.0.exe**.

3. Leave **Create an 8-bit desktop shortcut** checked if you want the pixel amplifier icon on your desktop. It is optional and checked by default.

4. Finish installation, then launch **Circus Genetic** from the desktop or Start menu.

Installation is for your Windows account and does not require an administrator password. The engine, artwork and music are included; you can play offline without a separate browser installation.

Uninstall through Windows Settings → Apps, or the uninstall shortcut in the Circus Genetic Start menu folder. Uninstalling keeps app profile data. The current run is not a saved game.

# Getting started

Click the red power switch on the amp, or press **Enter**. The Panta Rhei Productions startup leads into the animated title screen.

**Start New Band** begins a complete night. Choose your musician and work your way from garage practice to the final battle.

**Chapter Select** opens the old CD bin. Use **Left/Right** to flip through cases and **Enter** to pick a chapter, then choose your player. Clicking the menu buttons works too. Direct chapter entry is useful for practice, but does not qualify for the full-night perfect-score bonus.

- **Matt:** bass guitar.

- **Brice:** lead guitar and vocals.

- **Tony:** drums; fights with drumsticks.

Matt and Brice use their instruments in combat. Tony sits behind his kit when performing and carries his sticks when fighting.

# Controls

| Key | Action |
| - | - |
| WASD or arrow keys | Move during action sections |
| J | Instrument strike |
| K | Heavy strike |
| L | Dash during action sections |
| Space | Jump during action; timed release in party games |
| E | Interact or confirm |
| P | Pause or resume |
| M | Toggle music |
| Esc | Return to title during normal gameplay |
| F11 | Toggle full screen in the Windows app |
| X | Switch band members during the final battle |
| Enter | Start a new band after the game-over sequence |

The on-screen buttons provide the main controls too. If changing windows pauses the game, click back into it and press **P**.

## Rhythm sections

Tap the incoming graffiti arrows as they reach the hit line. Use the **arrow keys**, or **A/S/D/F** for **Left/Down/Up/Right**. These rhythm bindings differ from WASD movement. Better timing improves judgments and combos.

## Sound mixer

Use **Left/Right** to select a channel, **Up/Down** to adjust its gain and **E** to confirm. Bring all three meters into their green zones.

## Amp rescue

Run to the blue van and press **E** nearby to collect the spare amp. Return to the venue door and press **E** to deliver it.

## Groupie scorecards

Use **Left/Right** or **1–5** to set a rating, then **E** to save it. After rating everyone, use **Left/Right** or **1–3** to make one final choice and confirm with **E**.

The lucky choice is randomized each run. Higher star ratings do not guarantee it. A lucky pick grants **3× size and one-hit attacks**.

## Beer Pong

Use **Left/Right** to adjust aim. Press **Space** when both the direction and power readings suit the cup you want.

A cup on the left needs a leftward throw. Space locks both readings at that instant; holding it does not charge a stronger shot. Misses can bounce off the table or fall away. Successful throws land in a cup.

## Blowin’ O’s

Use **Up/Down** to adjust the smoke-ring angle. Press **Space** when the angle and cycling breath reading line up for your shot. The inhale/puff animation runs while you aim. This is a timing challenge, not a hold-to-charge control.

# Chapters

1. **Garage Practice:** rehearse with the full band and match notes on the beat. Your roommate and Tony’s blue heeler provide questionable artistic supervision.

2. **Soundcheck:** play your part, then fix the mix while the sound guy listens.

3. **The Show:** perform and clear stage trouble, including bad promoters, lousy openers, pompous headliners and beer-spilling fans.

4. **Amp Emergency:** retrieve a replacement amp from the blue van and get it back through the venue door.

5. **Groupie Scorecards:** rate the scorecards, then make one choice. A lucky pick awards giant power and a short power-up encore before the party.

6. **Afterparty:** take on Beer Pong and **Blowin’ O’s — Smoke Ring Olympics** while teammates cheer and partygoers make regrettable decisions.

7. **Battle of the Bands:** a living-room TV ad pulls you into a local dive bar. Defeat thirty rival musicians from ten bands.

## Final battle tips

The band shares one health meter. **X** switches between Matt, Brice and Tony; switching does not refill health.

- **Bruisers** guard light frontal attacks. Use **K** to break their guards.

- **Flankers** try to get around you. Keep moving and watch both sides.

- **Beer throwers** aim ahead of moving targets. Change direction, jump or dash, then close the distance.

Your uncontrolled bandmates help, but they will not win the whole fight for you. Giant power stays with the musician who earned it; switching control does not transfer it.

Crowd interruptions briefly freeze the brawl, at most three times per fight.

Earlier failures lead to recoveries, retries or rematches. Losing the final battle ends the career: you get kicked out of the band, the music fades into street noise, and **GAME OVER — YOU SUCK** flashes on screen. Let the kickout animation finish, then press **Enter** to start again. Esc is blocked during that animation.

Beat all thirty rivals to become **Town Champions**.

# The secret track

A full night with a perfect score, including the final battle, unlocks the hidden survival stage. Direct chapter play does not count as a perfect full-night run.

There is another way in: investigate the **VILLIAN** sticker on the chapter-selection CD box. It opens a six-blank password screen. A correct entry starts the secret stage automatically.

Your hint:

> Its home keeps moving, but its center has no corners.

No password is printed in this guide.

The secret stage is a survival run. Use **Space** or **Up** to jump. When the run ends, you return to the main game; it does not end your band’s career.

# Soundtrack

- **Villain’s Clear:** the main title and early-night practice, show and amp sequence.

- **Circus Genetic – Dice:** from Groupie Scorecards, including its power-up encore, through the Afterparty, Beer Pong and Blowin’ O’s sequence.

- **Circus Get High:** starts with the Battle of the Bands TV ad and continues through the fight and victory. Defeat fades it into traffic, heckles, sirens and chip tones.

- **All My Friends Are Dead:** reserved for the secret stage. Returning from that stage restores the appropriate main-game soundtrack.

# Troubleshooting and build notes

**No sound:** click inside the game, press **M** and check your computer’s volume.

**Game paused after changing windows:** click back into the game and press **P**.

**An action key seems inactive:** let the section title or cinematic finish, then check the instructions at the bottom of the game screen. Controls change with the activity.

**Want to return to a section:** use Chapter Select. This demo has no mid-level Save/Load feature. Closing or restarting the app does not preserve the current run in progress.

The installer is unsigned. Game checks, desktop-host checks and packaged-file verification passed during the build. Installation and execution on an actual Windows computer were not tested in the build environment.
