CIRCUS GENETIC — CONTINUOUS LIVE SHOW + GROUPIE ROULETTE UPDATE

WHAT IS IN THIS UPDATE

LIVE SHOW
One continuous 2,400-unit venue: stage, stage steps, crowd floor and sound desk,
bar, then exit door. The background is one unique panorama; it does not tile or
repeat the stage. Animated patrons occupy the crowd area behind the fighters.
Clear the stage wave, move right into the crowd, clear that wave and reach the
exit. After the amp run, clear the encore enemies through the venue again.

Your normal stage fighter is 168 game pixels high, at least as tall as the
performing guitarists. The existing walking, striking and weapon animations
scale with the fighter. Tony still fights with sticks only. The selected
musician is absent from their stage station during combat.

A flashing pixel sign reads KICK THE DRUNK FANS OFF STAGE at round start.
Reduced-motion mode keeps the sign steady.

THE AMP BLEW
The broken smoking amp is visible onstage. Press E to leave for the blue van.
Collect and return the replacement as before. Choosing the amp chapter now
also begins at the smoking amp, so the event is visible before the van run.

GROUPIE ROULETTE
The rating minigame and its labels have been removed from this edition.
Choose three distinct faces from nine. Your three choices load into a slot
machine. Each reel spins among those three faces and stops separately.

Three matching faces: +10,000 bonus points.
Exactly one hidden face among the nine carries giant power. If you included
her in your three picks, you get the giant prize after the spin, even if the
reels do not match. You do not have to land her on the payline.

There is one spin per run and no point cost. The jackpot and power are separate
awards. After the result, continue into the giant encore or the afterparty.
The camera zooms out for giant stage combat to show the 3x character.

CONTROLS
Choose faces: click, or 1-9, or arrows and E.
Remove a pick before all three are loaded: click it again or its number.
Change loaded picks: Backspace, or click a loaded face.
Pull lever: Space, E, Enter, or click the lever.
Continue after the result: E, Enter, Space, or click the lower result area.

UPLOAD TO YOUR EXISTING GITHUB GAME
1. Extract Circus-Genetic-Live-Show-and-Roulette-Update.zip.
2. Open your existing game repository on GitHub, on the Code tab.
3. Stay at the top level where index.html, game.html, assets/ and scripts/ live.
4. Choose Add file > Upload files.
5. Drag ALL CONTENTS of the extracted update folder into the upload area,
   preserving the assets, scripts and website folders. Do not upload the ZIP
   or put an extra outer update folder around its contents.
6. Commit changes. Example message: Add continuous live venue and Groupie Roulette
7. Wait for GitHub Pages deployment. Open the live Pages URL and press Ctrl+F5.

The included index.html is the updated Circus Genetic branded homepage.
The included game.html runs the updated game. The homepage Play buttons point
to game.html. These match the branded homepage supplied in the previous update.
The README, handbook, CD chapter name, homepage chapter preview and game guide
all use Groupie Roulette and explain its new rules.

If uploading in separate commits, upload assets FIRST, then scripts and website,
then the HTML and documentation files. Keep all existing unrelated files.

Do not delete or replace entire folders. These uploaded files merge into your
existing folders, replacing matching files and adding the new images. Your
existing music, styles.css, remaining game scripts and other assets are needed.
Do not create assets/assets or scripts/scripts.

This package also retains the previous animated Player Select and layered live
band assets, so it includes both artwork fixes from the last update.
